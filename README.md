《辨世人祀鬼論》是一張華文基督教論說印件，見哈佛燕京圖書館 DRS 428712045。館目著錄此件約於 1840 年代在廈門刊行，但確切刊年未明，作者不詳。版面框外上方印有由右至左的橫題《耶穌教簡要格言》，框內篇題為《辨世人祀鬼論》，正文直排，由右至左行文，末欄下方另刊「在厦刊送」。就內容而言，文本開首即以「只崇一造物上帝」作為立論起點。其後文本主張鬼既不能賜福與人，亦不能降禍，並據此批評向鬼行祭的做法，文中舉門首與墓前焚香叩拜為具體例子。篇末承前勸人悔改、倚賴耶穌，並以耶穌救罪作結。

Bian shi ren si gui lun (辨世人祀鬼論) is a single-sheet Chinese-language Christian tract held at the Harvard-Yenching Library (DRS 428712045). The item is catalogued as printed in Xiamen around the 1840s. The exact year of printing is not established, and the author is unknown. Above the frame, a horizontal heading reading right to left gives the general title Yesu jiao jianyao geyan (耶穌教簡要格言). Inside the frame, the piece is titled Bian shi ren si gui lun (辨世人祀鬼論). The main text runs in vertical columns from right to left, and a separate imprint below the final column reads “zai Xia kan song” (在厦刊送). In its argument, the text opens by taking the worship of only one Creator God (zaowu Shangdi) as its starting point. It then asserts that ghosts can neither bestow blessings on people nor send calamities upon them. On this basis it criticizes making offerings to ghosts, citing incense burning and kowtowing at doorways and before graves as concrete examples. The text closes by urging repentance and reliance on Jesus, ending with Jesus' salvation from sin.
